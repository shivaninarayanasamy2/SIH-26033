Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Idea: FARMCONNECT – Connecting Farmers Directly with Buyers
FARMCONNECT is a digital agricultural marketplace that connects farmers with retailers, wholesalers, restaurants, supermarkets and individual consumers through a single platform.

The main objective is to reduce unnecessary intermediaries, increase farmers' earnings and provide consumers with agricultural products at fair and transparent prices.

Instead of completely eliminating every intermediary, our solution replaces unnecessary middlemen with a transparent system that allows essential services such as transportation, storage, grading and packaging to continue.
How does FARMCONNECT work?
1. Farmer Registration: Farmers register using their mobile number and provide basic details about their location, crops and available produce.
2. Product Listing: Farmers upload details of their agricultural products, including quantity, expected harvest date, quality and expected price.
3. Buyer Connection: Retailers, supermarkets, restaurants and consumers can view available products and place orders directly.
4. Bulk Order Matching: The platform combines nearby buyers' requirements and connects them with farmers or Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).
5. Shared Transportation: Orders from nearby locations are grouped to reduce transportation costs.
6. Transparent Pricing: Farmers can see the buyer's price, transportation charges, service fees and their expected earnings before accepting an order.
7. Secure Payment: Payments are made through recognised digital payment methods, with transaction records available to both parties.
8. Delivery and Feedback: Products are delivered through coordinated logistics, and buyers can provide feedback on product quality.
What makes our idea unique?
The Demand-First Harvest Matching System.

Unlike an ordinary agricultural marketplace, FARMCONNECT focuses on collecting buyer demand before farmers transport their produce.

For example:
- Five restaurants require 100 kg of tomatoes.
- Three local retailers require another 150 kg.
- The platform identifies a nearby farmer or FPO with 250 kg of available tomatoes.
- It combines these requirements into one supply plan.
- Transportation is coordinated through a shared collection point.
This reduces unnecessary transportation, helps farmers identify actual demand and minimises the risk of unsold produce.

Our key innovation: We connect demand, farmers, quality verification and shared logistics in one coordinated system rather than simply creating another online buying and selling application.

The proposed system will be developed as a mobile application and web-based platform with a central database and cloud backend.

## Feasibility and Viability
The solution is practical because it uses existing technology and agricultural infrastructure.
- Technical: Uses mobile applications, cloud storage and GPS.
- Economic: Reduces unnecessary transportation and marketing expenses.
- Operational: Works with existing FPOs and collection centres.
- Financial: Can generate revenue through small transaction fees and optional buyer subscriptions.

## Conclusion
FARMCONNECT provides a practical digital solution to reduce unnecessary intermediaries in agricultural marketing.

By connecting farmers directly with buyers through smart demand matching, shared transportation and transparent pricing, it aims to improve farmers' earnings and make agricultural products more affordable for consumers.

Our Vision: Fair earnings for farmers, fair prices for consumers.
FARMCONNECT is not just an online agricultural marketplace. It is a demand-based farm-to-market system that connects buyers and farmers while reducing unnecessary supply-chain costs.
